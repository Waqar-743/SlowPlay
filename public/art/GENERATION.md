# Original AKI artwork

Created with the built-in image-generation tool, without CLI or API-key fallback. The reference informed composition and atmosphere; no reference screenshots or downloaded third-party paintings were used as source artwork.

## 1. Wide daylight — generation

Use case: stylized-concept. Original cinematic website hero background, no UI. Widescreen 16:9 Japanese autumn landscape, detailed painterly photorealistic cinematic matte painting, natural organic texture and subtle film grain. A peaceful samurai sits on a grassy hillside at the right, in three-quarter rear view, quietly looking toward distant snow-capped Mount Fuji. Understated charcoal/brown historical robes, small topknot, sheathed katana beside him; no action. Man around 73% across and 70% down, thick dark maple trunk near 89% across, rich crimson/vermilion canopy across upper-right third. Fuji's snowy summit around 53% across and 46% down, mountain layers fading into blue haze. Olive-green and golden grass along bottom quarter, hillside rising to the right, natural rocks. Left 40% stays clean pale blue sky and soft haze for HTML typography. Bright cool autumn daylight, soft directional sunlight, sophisticated crimson/green/blue palette. No airborne leaves (added in code), buildings, temples, extra people, text, symbols, logos, border, watermark or interface.

## 2. Wide dusk — edit of daylight

Use case: lighting-weather. Keep exact framing, placement and identity of samurai, maple, Fuji, mountain layers, lake, slope and rocks. Change only time and atmosphere to cinematic early blue hour: indigo-blue sky, desaturated peach afterglow on horizon, small delicate crescent high around 43% across and 16% down, a few subtle stars, cool mist, warm last sunlight on crimson leaves and grass, readable charcoal robes, cool moonlit snow. Keep the clean left-side negative space. Dusk remains detailed and readable, not near-black. No new objects, lights, buildings, people, airborne leaves, text or UI.

## 3. Portrait daylight — recompose wide daylight

Use case: compositing. Vertical 9:16 mobile companion preserving original character identity and art style. Upper-left two thirds, from y=5% to45%, is clean pale blue autumn sky for HTML copy. Crimson maple canopy mainly along upper-right edge, trunk far right. Fuji summit around x=43%, y=58%, mountain haze below. Full seated samurai around x=72%, y=78%, grassy hillside and sheathed katana beside him. Grass across bottom25%, natural rocks bottom-right, matching bright soft daylight. Both Fuji's summit and seated samurai visible without cropping. No airborne leaves, UI, writing, logos or watermarks.

## 4. Portrait dusk — edit portrait daylight

Use case: lighting-weather. Portrait daylight is the edit target; wide dusk is the lighting reference. Preserve exact portrait geometry, tree branches, character, Fuji and hillside. Change only daylight to the matching indigo early-blue-hour palette, peach horizon afterglow, cool haze, warm last sunlight on crimson foliage and grass, readable robes and moonlit snow. Small crescent near60% across and16% down, very subtle stars. Preserve upper-left clean sky. Same vertical aspect ratio, no added objects, airborne leaves, writing or UI.

## 5. Maple leaf — transparent generation

Use case: photorealistic-natural. One isolated Japanese red maple leaf, centred and mostly face-on with a slight natural curl. Seven sharply serrated lobes, slender petiole, fine real vein structure and papery surface. Rich crimson and burnt vermilion with restrained orange highlights, soft upper-left autumn daylight, natural irregular organic edges and tiny vein shadows. Leaf fills about85% of the square image, with padding and stem included. Genuine transparent alpha; no ground, external cast shadow, glow, border, text, watermark, additional leaves or objects. Crisp cutout suitable for rotation and flutter animation.

## Delivered files

- daylight.webp and dusk.webp: 1672 × 941 wide paintings.
- daylight-portrait.webp and dusk-portrait.webp: 941 × 1672 portrait paintings.
- maple-leaf.webp: 512 × 512 transparent particle artwork; its full-resolution PNG is preserved with the original paintings.
- Original PNGs are included in the separately delivered generated-assets archive.

Compression uses WebP quality90, without cropping or compositional edits. Particle leaf silhouettes, grass and haze are rendered independently in code.
